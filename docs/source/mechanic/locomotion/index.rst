Locomotion
==========

Motors
######

The drivetrain uses 24 V brushed DC motors described as clones of the Maxon RE40.

Reduction
#########

A single-stage gear reduction provides a ratio of **3.7:1** between the motor shaft and the wheel shaft.

Wheels
######

The robot uses vulcanized wheels supplied by JSumo, with nominal dimensions of 45 mm in diameter and 30 mm in width.
